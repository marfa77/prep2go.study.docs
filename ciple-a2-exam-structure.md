# CIPLE A2 Exam – Quick Facts

- **Level:** A2 (CEFR)
- **Full name:** Certificado Inicial de Português Língua Estrangeira
- **Authority:** CAPLE (Universidade de Lisboa)
- **Required for citizenship:** Yes (Portugal)
- **Required for residency:** Yes (Portugal)
- **Language:** European Portuguese (PT-PT), not Brazilian

## Sections

| Section | Duration | Weight | Format |
|---------|----------|--------|--------|
| Reading + Writing | 1h 15min | 45% | Multiple choice, correspondence, short texts (40–100 words) |
| Listening | 30 min | 30% | Audio, multiple choice, true/false |
| Speaking | 10–15 min | 25% | Face-to-face, introduce yourself, describe pictures, discuss topics |

## Pass requirements

- **Overall:** 55% minimum
- **Per section:** 35% minimum in each
- Fail one section → retake only that section

## Total duration

~2 hours (excluding breaks)

- Check [caple.letras.ulisboa.pt](https://caple.letras.ulisboa.pt) for exam sessions and registration
