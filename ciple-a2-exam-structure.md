# CIPLE A2 Exam – Quick Facts

- **Level:** A2 (CEFR)
- **Full name:** Certificado Inicial de Português Língua Estrangeira
- **Authority:** CAPLE (Universidade de Lisboa)
- **Citizenship:** accepted proof of A2 Portuguese for Portuguese nationality applications
- **Residence permits:** not a general requirement; check AIMA for your specific route
- **Language:** European Portuguese (PT-PT), not Brazilian

## Sections

| Section | Duration | Weight | Format |
|---------|----------|--------|--------|
| Reading + Writing | 1h 15min | 45% | Multiple choice, correspondence, short texts (40–100 words) |
| Listening | 30 min | 30% | Audio, multiple choice, true/false |
| Speaking | ~15 min | 25% | Face-to-face, introduce yourself, describe pictures, discuss topics |

## Pass requirements

- **Pass:** 55% overall (CAPLE scale 55–100%)
- No per-section minimum

## Total duration

~2 hours (excluding breaks)

## Fee and 2026 dates

- **Fee:** €95 (CAPLE, non-refundable)
- **November 2026 international sitting:** 5 November 2026 (7 November 2026 at LAPE-FLUL, Lisbon)
- **Registration closes:** 20 October 2026
- Other centres and national (CIPLE-P) sittings: see [caple.letras.ulisboa.pt](https://caple.letras.ulisboa.pt/exame/2/ciple)
