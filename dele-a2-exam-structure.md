# DELE A2 Exam – Quick Facts

- **Level:** A2 (CEFR)
- **Full name:** Diploma de Español como Lengua Extranjera
- **Authority:** Instituto Cervantes (Spanish Ministry of Education)
- **Required for:** Spanish residency and citizenship
- **Language:** Spanish (Spain)

## Sections (post-2020 format)

| Section | Duration | Tasks | Format |
|---------|----------|-------|--------|
| Reading | 60 min | 4 tasks, 25 questions | Everyday texts, multiple choice |
| Listening | 40 min | 4 tasks, 25 questions | Everyday audio, played twice |
| Writing | 45 min | 2 tasks | Email reply, short text 70–80 words |
| Speaking | 12 min + 12 min prep | 3 tasks | Monologue, photo description, conversation |

## Pass requirements

- **Total:** 100 points
- **Minimum:** 50/100 overall; minimum score per paper (check official rules)
- **Retakes:** Individual papers can be retaken

## Total duration

~2.5 hours (written papers) + speaking slot

## Key dates

- Check [examenes.cervantes.es](https://examenes.cervantes.es) for sessions and registration
