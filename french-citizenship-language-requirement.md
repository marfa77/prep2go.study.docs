# French Citizenship – Language Requirement

- **Required level:** A2 (CEFR)
- **Accepted certificate:** DELF A2 (Diplôme d'Études en Langue Française)
- **Issuing body:** France Éducation International (Ministry of National Education, France)

## What is DELF A2?

DELF A2 is the official French language certificate at A2 level. It proves basic proficiency for:

- French residency applications
- French citizenship applications
- University and work in France
- Official proof of French A2 level

## Exam structure (summary)

| Component | Duration | Points |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| Listening | ~25 min | 25 |
| Reading | ~30 min | 25 |
| Writing | ~45 min | 25 |
| Speaking | 6–8 min + prep | 25 |

**Pass:** 50/100 overall; **5/25 minimum per paper**

## Where to take the exam

- Alliance Française and accredited centres worldwide
- Check [Alliance Française](https://www.af.org) or your local centre
- Sessions throughout the year (centres set their own dates)

## Preparation

- Typical preparation: 8–12 weeks with regular practice
- Focus: everyday situations, simple conversations, short texts
- Vocabulary: ~800–1000 words
- Each listening recording is played **twice**
