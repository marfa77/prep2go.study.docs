# CELI 2 Exam – Quick Facts

- **Level:** B1 (CEFR) — *Note: CELI 1 = A2; CELI 2 = B1*
- **Full name:** Certificato di Conoscenza della Lingua Italiana
- **Authority:** Università per Stranieri di Perugia
- **Required for:** Italian residency and citizenship (B1 accepted for some routes)
- **Language:** Italian

## Sections

| Section | Duration | Format |
|---------|----------|--------|
| Reading (Prova A) | ~60 min | 5 short texts, gap-fills, comprehension |
| Writing (Prova B) | ~60 min | 3 tasks: questionnaire + 2 short texts (~90–100 words) |
| Listening (Prova C) | ~20 min | 3 audio prompts, multiple choice |
| Speaking | ~10–12 min + prep | Self-intro, role-play, describe visual + questions |

## Pass requirements

- Aggregate score across all four skills
- Minimum per paper may apply (check centre rules)
- Partial retakes possible at some centres

## Total duration

~2h 20 (written) + speaking slot (~10–12 min)

## Key dates

- Sessions several times per year (University of Perugia calendar)
- Register 30–40 days in advance
- Check [cvcl.it](https://www.cvcl.it) or your exam centre
