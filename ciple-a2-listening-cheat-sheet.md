# CIPLE A2 Listening – Quick Facts

- **Section:** Compreensão do Oral (Listening Comprehension)
- **Exam:** CIPLE A2 (Portuguese citizenship)
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Weight:** 30% of total

## Format

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Content | Informal speeches, everyday situations |
| Tasks | Multiple choice, correspondence, true/false |
| Topics | Greetings, personal info, directions, transport, health, post office, invitations, telephone messages |

## What you need to understand

- Simple social remarks (greeting, thanking, congratulating)
- Routine questions at border posts
- Information about places, times, dates
- Instructions on accommodation, food, health
- Basic info at post offices and banks
- Automated telephone messages

## Tips

1. **European Portuguese only** — PT-PT, not Brazilian; pronunciation differs
2. Practice with real CAPLE-style audio (conversations, announcements)
3. Focus on key words; don't try to catch every syllable
4. Read questions before each recording starts
5. Use the 35% minimum per section rule — you don't need to ace it
