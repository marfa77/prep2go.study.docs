# DELF A2 Exam – Quick Facts

- **Level:** A2 (CEFR)
- **Full name:** Diplôme d'Études en Langue Française
- **Authority:** France Éducation International (Ministry of National Education, France)
- **Required for:** French residency and citizenship
- **Language:** French (France)

## Sections

| Section | Duration | Points | Format |
|---------|----------|--------|--------|
| Listening (Compréhension orale) | ~25 min | 25 | 3–4 recordings, each played twice |
| Reading (Compréhension écrite) | ~30 min | 25 | 3–5 short texts, everyday life |
| Writing (Production écrite) | ~45 min | 25 | 2 short texts (message, letter, invite, etc.) |
| Speaking (Production orale) | 6–8 min + ~10 min prep | 25 | 3 parts: conversation, monologue, role play |

## Pass requirements

- **Total:** 100 points (25 per paper)
- **Minimum:** 50/100 overall; **5/25 per paper**
- Fail one paper → retake only that paper

## Total duration

~1h 40 (written) + speaking slot (~15–20 min with prep)

- Check [Alliance Française](https://www.af.org) or your local exam centre for sessions
