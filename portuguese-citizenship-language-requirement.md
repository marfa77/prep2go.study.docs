# Portuguese Citizenship – Language Requirement

- **Required level:** A2 (CEFR)
- **Accepted certificate:** CIPLE A2 (Certificado Inicial de Português Língua Estrangeira)
- **Issuing body:** CAPLE (Centro de Avaliação e Certificação de Português Língua Estrangeira), Universidade de Lisboa

## What is CIPLE A2?

CIPLE A2 is the official Portuguese language certificate at A2 level. It proves basic proficiency for:

- Portuguese nationality (citizenship) applications
- Residence permits: not a general requirement; check AIMA for your specific route
- Official proof of Portuguese A2 level

## Exam structure (summary)

| Component | Duration | Weight |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| Reading + Writing | 1h 15min | 45% |
| Listening | 30 min | 30% |
| Speaking | ~15 min | 25% |

**Pass:** 55% overall, no per-section minimum (CAPLE)

**Fee:** €95 (CAPLE)

## Language variant

- **European Portuguese (PT-PT)** is required
- Brazilian Portuguese differs in vocabulary, pronunciation, and some grammar
- Exam uses European Portuguese only

## Where to take the exam

- CAPLE centres worldwide: [caple.letras.ulisboa.pt](https://caple.letras.ulisboa.pt)
- Sessions: national (Portugal, CIPLE-P) + international (May, July, November)
- November 2026: 5 November (7 November at LAPE-FLUL, Lisbon); registration closes 20 October 2026

## Preparation

- Typical preparation: 8–12 weeks for candidates with some prior exposure
- From zero: 3–6 months with regular practice
- Focus: everyday situations (AIMA, shops, transport, health, housing)
