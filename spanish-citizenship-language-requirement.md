# Spanish Citizenship – Language Requirement

- **Required level:** A2 (CEFR)
- **Accepted certificate:** DELE A2 (Diploma de Español como Lengua Extranjera)
- **Issuing body:** Instituto Cervantes (on behalf of the Spanish Ministry of Education)

## What is DELE A2?

DELE A2 is the official Spanish language certificate at A2 level. It proves basic proficiency for:

- Spanish residency applications
- Spanish citizenship applications
- University access in Spain
- Official proof of Spanish A2 level

## Exam structure (summary)

| Component | Duration |
|-----------|----------|
| Reading | 60 min |
| Listening | 40 min |
| Writing | 45 min |
| Speaking | 12 min + 12 min prep |

**Pass:** 50/100 overall; minimum per paper (check official rules)

## Where to take the exam

- Instituto Cervantes centres worldwide: [examenes.cervantes.es](https://examenes.cervantes.es)
- Multiple sessions per year (e.g. May, July, November)

## Preparation

- Typical preparation: 8–12 weeks with regular practice
- Focus: everyday situations, simple texts, basic conversations
- Vocabulary: ~800–1000 words
