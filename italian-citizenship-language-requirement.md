# Italian Citizenship – Language Requirement

- **Required level:** A2 or B1 (CEFR), depending on route
- **Accepted certificates:** CELI 1 (A2), CELI 2 (B1), CILS, PLIDA, or other recognized exams
- **Issuing body (CELI):** Università per Stranieri di Perugia

## CELI levels

| CELI | CEFR | Use case |
|------|------|----------|
| CELI 1 | A2 | Residence, some citizenship routes |
| CELI 2 | B1 | Citizenship (marriage, residence), university |

## CELI 1 (A2) structure (summary)

- **Reading + Writing:** ~120 min (gap-fill, guided text 70–80 words)
- **Listening:** ~20 min
- **Speaking:** ~10 min

## CELI 2 (B1) structure (summary)

- **Reading:** ~60 min
- **Writing:** ~60 min (3 tasks, ~90–100 words each)
- **Listening:** ~20 min
- **Speaking:** ~10–12 min + prep

## Where to take the exam

- CELI centres worldwide: [cvcl.it](https://www.cvcl.it)
- University of Perugia calendar — several sessions per year
- Register 30–40 days in advance

## Preparation

- A2: 8–12 weeks; B1: 12–16 weeks with regular practice
- Focus: everyday Italian, simple texts, basic conversations
